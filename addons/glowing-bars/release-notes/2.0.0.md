## Glowing Bars 2.0.0

### New

- **Basic and Advanced views**: the `/gb` table opens with the columns most people need. The **Advanced** button at the top shows them all. What you set in a hidden column keeps working.
- **When column**: for each type or spell, show it always, in combat, out of combat, or only with an enemy targeted. Low reagents and low stock can also be **Alert in town**: the glow shows anywhere, and the message and sound only in a town or an inn.
- **Glow time column**: how long the glow and the message show, from 1 to 10 seconds, or until done.
- **Animation column**: circling, breathing or none, for each type or spell.
- More cooldowns glow when they are ready: Bloodrage, Sweeping Strikes, Berserker Rage, Retaliation, Preparation, Evocation, Ice Barrier, Nature's Swiftness and Death Coil.
- Small headings for each type in the list of spells, and tooltips on the column headers.
- The table shrinks to fit if your screen is too narrow for it.

### Changed

- **Your settings from earlier versions carry over.**
- **Repeat** now brings back the glow as well as the sound and the message.
- **Low reagents and low stock now work the same way.** The limit column is called **Below**: at 10 it stays quiet, at 9 it warns. Low reagents warn below 10 by default, and low stock below 20. A limit you set yourself still warns at the same count.
- The low reagent message follows **Glow time** and **Repeat** like any other message.
- The cooldown settings moved from the Settings page to the table: *Also out of combat* is now the **When** column, and the glow time is now the **Glow time** column.
- The *Glow animation* setting moved to the **Animation** column. On the Settings page, only *Circling on round buttons* stays.

### Fixed

- A Breathing or None glow could show a large grid of images after you logged in.
- A cooldown set to show always now also glows at the start of a fight when you ask for that.
