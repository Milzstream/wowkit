## Glowing Bars 1.9.0

### New

- **Low stock**: add any item by its Item ID in the `/gb` table (arrows, water, candles). When only a few are left, a
  message says how many, even if the item is not on your bars. If it is on your bars, its button glows too.
- **Low at column**: set in the table how few is low, for each item and reagent or for the whole type.
- **Repeat on every type**: every type of reminder can now repeat its sound and message.
- Tooltips show the **Item ID** when _Show spell IDs in tooltips_ is on.

### Changed

- The reagent limit moved from the Settings page to the Low at column. The limit you set is kept.
- A message only comes back if its Repeat is on. Before, it came back every 30 seconds.
- If the type you pick is wrong for a spell, you get an error. Before, the spell could be added as another type.
- Food and drink can only be added as a missing buff.

### Fixed

- You can add more than one food or drink when their spells have the same name, and each has its own look. A look you
  set on a food or drink before goes back to the default, so set it again.
- The low reagent message now repeats together with its sound in towns, and also names the reagents of spells you added.
