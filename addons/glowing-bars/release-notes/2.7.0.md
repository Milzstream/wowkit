## Glowing Bars 2.7.0

### New

- Shield Block glows for warriors in a fight while it is not up. It comes back when its 7 seconds are over, or as soon as your blocks have used it up and it is off cooldown. It glows only when you can use it: in Defensive Stance, with a shield and enough rage.

### Changed

- A missing buff glows only when you can cast it now and it is off its cooldown. This goes for the buffs you added too.

### Fixed

- Revenge now glows after a normal block too, not only after a dodge, a parry or a full block.
