## Glowing Bars 1.8.0

### New

- **Food and drink as buffs**: turn on _Show spell IDs in tooltips_, put the item on your bars, and add the _Use spell ID_
  from its tooltip in the `/gb` table as a missing buff. Only that item glows, under its own name in the table, and not
  when you have none left. A food or drink stops glowing once you have its Well Fed buff.

### Changed

- The addon no longer keeps quiet while you eat or drink.
