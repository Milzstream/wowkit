## Glowing Bars 2.1.0

### Changed

- Nothing makes a sound by default any more. Old ranks and weapon buffs used to play an alarm; if you want a sound, pick one in the table.
- The buttons in the table now open a list of choices, so you pick what you want in one click: Sound, Repeat, When, Glow time, Animation, and the type when you add a spell or item. Only the sound you pick plays.
