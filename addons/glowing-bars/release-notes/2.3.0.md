## Glowing Bars 2.3.0

### New

- Overpower and Revenge glow in any stance, so you know when to switch. Overpower glows after your target dodges, Revenge after you block, dodge or parry.

### Changed

- The addon uses much less memory and CPU. It checks your bars twice a second instead of five times, and does less work each time.

### Fixed

- A food or drink you added as a buff no longer glows in every fight while you are Well Fed.
- Old spell ranks no longer show for spells on the gamepad bars when you don't play in gamepad mode. This could happen to druids in bear form.
- A buff that was only on the hidden gamepad bars now gets its reminder.
