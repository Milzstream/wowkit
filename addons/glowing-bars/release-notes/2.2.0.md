## Glowing Bars 2.2.0

### New

- Use your own sounds. Make a folder named `GlowingBars_Sounds` in `Interface\AddOns`, next to the addon's folder, and put your files in it, named `custom1`, `custom2` and so on, as `.ogg` or `.mp3`. Open the table with /gb, scroll to the bottom, and under Custom sounds enter how many there are. They then appear in the Sound list. With Add folder you can use more folders; type /reload after adding one.
